About Urban Pace · 어반페이스

Seoul-Born.
Street-Ready.

Urban Pace (어반페이스) is a Seoul-based sportswear brand creating fashionable running gear for people who stay active without sacrificing style. Born from the energy of Korea's streets — from Han River trail runs at dawn to Hongdae's electric midnight scene — every piece is designed for runners who demand both performance and aesthetic.

📍 HQ: Mapo-gu, Seoul, Republic of Korea

For runners who believe that style & performance go hand in hand.
